# ORG-002: Rich grounds and money tracking

Status: COMPLETE
Team: Lead
Services: Ground Booking, Tournaments
Depends on: Existing organisation accounts and transactional service state

## Request

Expand ground setup to match tournaments and include pricing and payment
tracking in both. The user confirmed pricing and tracking as the money scope.

## Acceptance criteria

- Organisations can add grounds from the directory or workspace with a
  description, surface, capacity, facilities, rules, contact, custom details
  and booking questions.
- Ground bookings show hourly hire, refundable deposit, total, payment
  instructions and refund policy before confirmation. Required and select
  booking answers are validated by the server and visible only to the owning
  player and organisation.
- Tournament setup includes entry fee, numeric cash prize pool, prizes,
  payment method, instructions and refund policy.
- Server-created bookings and registrations freeze agreed amounts and terms;
  players cannot supply payment totals or mark themselves paid.
- Owners record received payments and completed refunds, with optional
  references. Paid cancellations become refunds due until recorded returned.
  After a paid booking ends, owners can separately record its returned deposit.
- Owner dashboards show received and outstanding money for both services and
  pending refunds and deposits awaiting return. Players see payment states and
  cancelled registration history.
- A booking quote is checked within the reservation transaction. If the venue's
  prices, payment terms or booking questions change while a form is open, the
  booking is rejected until the player reviews refreshed details and reselects
  a slot.
- Existing working-tree changes and deployment configuration are preserved.
- Keep the established UI layout, navigation and arena login artwork. Ground
  setup uses the existing compact dialog; payment totals expand within the
  existing overview aside rather than adding a dashboard panel.

## Contract

Existing cookie authentication, CSRF checks, roles and transactional state apply.
New input is optional for compatibility with existing grounds and events.
Amounts are integer INR. Ground `pricePerHour` and `deposit` and tournament
`entryFee` are 0–100000; tournament `prizePool` is 0–10000000.
`paymentMethod` is `arrange_with_organiser`, `cash`, `upi` or `bank_transfer`;
`paymentInstructions` and `refundPolicy` each allow 1000 characters.

Ground `customDetails` uses the existing tournament label/value schema.
`bookingQuestions` uses the existing registration question schema: a stable
`id`, `label`, `type` (`text` or `select`), `required`, and select `options`.
`POST /api/bookings` accepts `{groundId,startsAt,answers?,notes?,quoteToken?}` and returns
`{booking}` with server-calculated `price`, `deposit`, `totalAmount`, `currency`,
`paymentStatus` and snapshot terms/questions. Tournament registration returns
the equivalent entry fee/payment snapshot. Answers and references are private.

Owner-only `PATCH /api/bookings/{id}/payment` and
`PATCH /api/tournaments/{id}/registrations/{registrationId}/payment` accept
`{paymentStatus:"paid"|"refunded",paymentReference?:string}`. Received payment
requires an active unpaid record; a full refund requires a cancelled record with
`refund_due`. Free entries and internal tournament venue reservations are excluded.
Booking payments additionally accept `paymentStatus:"deposit_refunded"` after
the paid booking ends; the booking remains paid with `depositRefunded:true` and
optional refund reference. These operations record direct transactions.

`GET /api/organisations/me` returns `payments`:
`{groundCollected,groundOutstanding,tournamentCollected,tournamentOutstanding,refundDue,depositReturnDue,currency}`.
Collected ground amounts subtract separately returned deposits. Owner events
expose private `registrations`; player events expose their cancelled
`myRegistrationHistory`.

Public grounds and availability include a `quoteToken` calculated from
server-owned prices, deposit, payment settings and booking questions.
Availability also returns the latest public `ground` to refresh displayed money
and form questions. The current UI sends that token when booking; a mismatch
returns 409 without writing a reservation. Omission supports existing API clients.
Paid cancellations use full-refund tracking, explained beside the policy field.
